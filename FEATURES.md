# 📱 Phone Mockup Tool - Available Features

A comprehensive list of all features available in the Phone Mockup Generator application.

---

## 🎨 **Phone Models & Customization**

### Phone Model Selection
- **Multiple Device Support**: Switch between iPhone 15 Pro and Samsung Galaxy S21
- **Model-Specific Settings**: Each model has optimized camera positions and screen resolutions
- **Automatic Configuration**: Camera and settings automatically adjust when switching models

### Phone Color Customization
- **iPhone Colors**: 
  - Black Titanium (default)
  - Natural Titanium
  - Blue Titanium
  - White Titanium
  - Orange
  - Navy Blue
- **Samsung Colors**:
  - Phantom Black (default)
  - Phantom White
  - Phantom Violet
  - Phantom Pink
  - Phantom Green
  - Phantom Gray
- **Realistic Materials**: iPhone uses advanced physical materials with metallic finishes and clearcoat for shininess
- **Dynamic Material Properties**: Material properties automatically adjust based on color brightness
- **Color Tooltips**: Hover over color presets to see color names with smart positioning

### Screen Customization
- **Screenshot Upload**: Upload and apply custom screenshots to phone screens
- **Multiple Screenshots**: Upload and manage multiple screenshots in a gallery
- **Screenshot Gallery**: View all uploaded screenshots and switch between them
- **Clear All**: Remove all uploaded screenshots at once
- **Resolution Information**: Display optimal resolution for each phone model

## 🎬 **Picture Mode**

### Camera Controls
- **Interactive 3D View**: Rotate, zoom, and pan the phone in 3D space
- **Mouse Controls**:
  - Left click + drag: Rotate the phone
  - Right click + drag: Pan the view
  - Scroll wheel: Zoom in/out
- **Camera Sliders**: Precise control over X, Y, and Z camera positions
- **Reset Camera**: Instantly return to default centered position
- **Reset Zoom**: Reset zoom level to default
- **Smart Rotation Limits**: Prevents viewing the back of the phone

### Background Customization
- **Preset Colors**: Quick selection of White, Light Gray, and Dark backgrounds
- **Multicolor Option**: Custom color picker for any background color
- **Real-time Preview**: See background changes instantly

### Export
- **High-Quality PNG Export**: Download your mockup as a PNG image
- **Full Resolution**: Export at maximum quality

## 🎥 **Video Mode**

### Animation System
- **Keyframe-Based Animation**: Create smooth camera movements with keyframes
- **Draggable Keyframes**: Move keyframes left and right on the timeline with mouse
- **Snap to Intervals**: Keyframes snap to 0.1-second intervals for precision
- **Unlimited Keyframes**: Add as many keyframes as needed (up to 10 seconds duration)
- **Real-time Preview**: Preview animations before exporting

### Timeline Controls
- **Play/Pause**: Control animation playback
- **Time Scrubbing**: Click anywhere on timeline to jump to that time
- **Duration Control**: Adjust video duration (1-10 seconds)
- **Visual Timeline**: See all keyframes and their positions at a glance

### Easing Options
- **6 Easing Types**:
  - Ease-in
  - Ease-out
  - Ease-in-out (default)
  - Bouncy
  - Soft bouncy
  - Gentle
- **Per-Segment Easing**: Set different easing for each animation segment
- **Visual Easing Bars**: See easing type between keyframes with easy selection

### Export Frame
- **Aspect Ratio Selection**: Choose from Square, 16:9, 9:16, or 4:3
- **Frame Zoom Controls**: Zoom in/out on the export frame
- **Resizable Frame**: Visual frame shows exactly what will be exported
- **Transparent Background**: Option to export with transparent background

### Video Export
- **MP4 Format**: Export animations as MP4 video files
- **60 FPS**: Smooth 60 frames per second export
- **Progress Tracking**: See export progress in real-time
- **High Quality**: Professional-grade video output

## 📋 **Quick Reference**

### Keyboard & Mouse Shortcuts
- **Left Click + Drag**: Rotate phone
- **Right Click + Drag**: Pan view
- **Scroll Wheel**: Zoom in/out
- **Click Timeline**: Jump to time in video mode
- **Drag Keyframe**: Move keyframe position

### Recommended Workflow
1. Select your phone model
2. Choose phone color
3. Upload screenshot(s)
4. Adjust camera position
5. Set background color
6. Export as image (Picture Mode) or create animation (Video Mode)

## 🎨 **Best Practices**

### Screenshot Guidelines
- **iPhone 15 Pro**: Upload images at 393×852 pixels for perfect crispness
- **Samsung S21**: Upload images at 360×800 pixels for perfect crispness
- **Format**: PNG or JPG supported
- **Size**: Under 10MB recommended

### Video Creation Tips
- Start with 2-3 keyframes for simple animations
- Use easing to create natural movement
- Preview frequently to check animation flow
- Keep duration under 10 seconds for best performance
